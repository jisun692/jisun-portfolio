03 / A little about me

Curious by nature.
Precise by practice.

I’m Ushal Ahmed Jisun, a graphics designer and video editor with 4+ years of experience shaping ideas into clear, memorable visuals.

I enjoy working with ambitious founders, creative teams, and growing brands—especially when there’s room to explore. My process is hands-on and experimental: try directions, learn from what works, and refine until the right idea clicks. My remote agency experience includes Brand Clamp Inc, where I worked as a Graphics Designer and Video Editor, and NRLIT, where I worked as a Graphics Designer and Jr Web Developer (2024–25). I’ve been working with clients on Upwork since 2023. Some client work stays private under NDA.
